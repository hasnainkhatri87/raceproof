# Code of conduct

RaceProof is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community.

Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, give actionable technical feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports should be made privately to the conduct contact the repository owner publishes before launch. Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical, with attention to the safety of affected people.
